# Time-Travelling Alien General
## Abstract
A history-themed geography game where players travel through key moments of World War I and World War II to locate relevant countries on a world map. Our target audience for this game are high schoolers that are taking a World History class, this game should flesh out their understanding and knowledge of the two World Wars. 

## The Game
### Storyline
You’re playing as an alien war general who goes back in time, trying to send their troops to specific locations on Earth. Your alien overlord gives you a mission to recreate the events of WWI and WWII.

### Learn Mode
Use the learn mode to explore different parts of the world map and learn about each country's involvements in WWI and WWII, giving players some context before they challenge themselves in play mode.

### Play Mode
Identify the target country on the map using your knowledge of history and geography to proceed with each stage. Complete all stages of the WWI or WWII level to prove yourself as a compotent alien war general.

### How to Run the Game
```
npm run dev

```
